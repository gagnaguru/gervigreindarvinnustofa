# Pokemon type chart (Gen 6+)

For each attacker type, super-effective (2×), not-very-effective (0.5×), and no-effect (0×) targets. Anything not listed is 1.0×.

For a dual-type defender, multiply the per-type values. (Example: Water against Rock/Ground = 2.0 × 2.0 = 4.0.)

For a dual-type attacker, you usually want the BEST of its two attacking types against the defender. (Example: Charizard is Fire/Flying. Fire vs Water is 0.5; Flying vs Water is 1.0; the best Charizard has is 1.0.)
