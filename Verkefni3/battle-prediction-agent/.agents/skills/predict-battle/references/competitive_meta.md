# Competitive Pokemon meta — Smogon OU tier digest (Gen 9)

> This document is provided for **lore context only**. The harness scores battles using the deterministic formula in `damage_formula.md` and does NOT consider competitive viability, EVs, IVs, abilities, items, or tier placement. Do not let this file influence numeric predictions.

## What is OU?

"OverUsed" — the most common competitive singles tier on Smogon. Pokemon banned from OU are categorized into Ubers, AG (Anything Goes), etc. OU usage is calculated from monthly ladder statistics on Pokemon Showdown.

## Notable S-tier Pokemon (Gen 9 OU, late 2024)

- **Kingambit** — Defiant + Supreme Overlord makes it terrifying in the late game. Iron Head spam with a Black Glasses.
- **Gholdengo** — Make It Rain spam under Choice Specs. Immune to status moves via Good As Gold.
- **Great Tusk** — Knock Off, Headlong Rush, Rapid Spin in one slot. Pivots into anything physical.
- **Iron Valiant** — Mixed attacker with Quark Drive boost. Choice Scarf Moonblast is a one-shot on most special walls.
- **Dragapult** — Speed tier of 142 + Dragon Darts + U-turn. Set variety: Specs, Choice Band, Substitute, Hex.
- **Landorus-Therian** — Intimidate utility, Earthquake/U-turn pivot. Defensive sets run Stealth Rock.
- **Heatran** — Magma Storm trapping sets, Specs Fire Blast, Stealth Rock support.

## Cultural notes

- "Stallbreaker" — Pokemon that beat defensive teams. Mixed Hoopa-U, Sub-Toxic Heatran, Taunt Tornadus-T.
- "Speed tier" matters more than raw stats for most matchups; outspeeding by 1 base often decides the game.
- The current generation introduced Tera Types, which can flip type matchups mid-battle.
- Choice Scarf changes the optimal speed tier for offensive Pokemon.

## Smogon tier abbreviations

- AG — Anything Goes
- Ubers — banned from OU but allowed in Ubers
- OU — OverUsed (the standard tier)
- UU — UnderUsed
- RU — RarelyUsed
- NU — NeverUsed
- PU — even more rarely used than NU
- LC — Little Cup (only first-stage, level 5 Pokemon)

## Iconic moments in competitive Pokemon history

- 2014 World Championships final: Pachirisu wins the world. Truly nothing was the same.
- The Garchomp ban from OU in DPP that birthed the term "uberization".
- Mega-Salamence's brief tenure in Gen 6 OU before getting banned to Ubers within months.
